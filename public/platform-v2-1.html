! Licence required for this category
Bathroom Renovation involving consent or restricted building work requires a Licensed Building Practitioner (LBP) with Site Supervisor 2 or higher classification. Plumbing and electrical work must be carried out by licensed plumbers and electricians.

TradieCheck will flag interested tradies’ licence status against these requirements. The decision on who to invite remains yours.
